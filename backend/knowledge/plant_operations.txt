INTELLIOPS PLANT OPERATIONS KNOWLEDGE

Plant NP001 is a production facility used for material processing and packaging.

Production:
The normal production target for Plant NP001 is 1000 units per shift.
A production result below 800 units per shift is considered low production.

Maintenance:
If a production line stops unexpectedly, the operator should first check the machine status and maintenance alerts.
Unplanned machine downtime can reduce production output.

Quality:
Finished products must pass the quality inspection before dispatch.
Products that fail inspection must be separated and reported to the quality team.

Safety:
Operators must follow plant safety procedures and wear the required personal protective equipment (PPE).
Any safety incident must be reported to the plant supervisor immediately.
